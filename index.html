Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost?

Costs vary based on scope, number of pages, and functionality. We offer competitive, transparent fixed-price quotes tailored for small and medium businesses without hidden recurring fees.

How long does it take to launch?

Most standard business websites or landing pages are designed, coded, and launched within 1 to 3 weeks depending on content readiness.

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes! We specialize in updating outdated sites into fast, modern, and accessible web experiences while preserving your existing search engine rankings.

Can you help with hosting and domain setup?

Absolutely. We help configure cost-effective hosting (like GitHub Pages or Netlify) and link your domain name (.ax, .se, .fi, or .com) hassle-free.
